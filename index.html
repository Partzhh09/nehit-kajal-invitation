Meet the Couple

A chance connection, mutual friends, and a little bit of destiny brought Nehit & Kajal together. Their story began at a mandir in Dubai, where destiny first brought them together. As Navratri arrived, the magic of Garba added a little rhythm to their growing connection. From coffee dates and dinner conversations to countless journeys between two cities, every moment brought them closer, until they found in each other a love that felt like home. When their parents finally met and said “Yes!”, two worlds became one. With the blessings of their families, Nehit & Kajal are ready to begin their greatest adventure yet — a lifetime of love, laughter, and dancing through life together.
